# Island Operations talk at PART

On Tuesday evening, October 20, 2026, I gave a talk at the monthly meeting of
[PART of Westford](https://www.wb1gof.org/), my local radio club. The meeting had an island
activation theme. I went first with "Island Operations: Activating Peddocks Island in Boston Harbor," and
Adam (AA1N) and James (NV1U) followed with their Cross Island, Maine expedition.

![The meeting agenda on the projector screen: Introductions, Club Reports and News, Election of Officers, then Island Activations with Chris W1YTQ on Peddocks Island in Boston Harbor and Adam AA1N and James NV1U on Cross Island, ME](PART_agenda.jpg){: width="600px"}

The talk ran about 20 minutes, and members in the room and on Zoom saw the slides. Adam took this photo of me presenting the first slide.

![Chris Farnham (W1YTQ) at a podium with a microphone, presenting a slide titled "Why islands?" on a projector screen with the Zoom attendees along the top](PART_talk_Adam_photo.jpg){: width="600px"}

> A short trip feels like a small expedition.

## My talk

I covered operating from an island and how you can do it too.

 - [Boston Harbor Islands as a POTA site]({% link _sections/POTA_Paddocks_Island.md %}) and how few people activate Peddocks Island ([US-2421](https://pota.app/#/park/US-2421))
 - How my troop gets to the island by private charter, plus the passenger ferry and paddling alternatives
 - Camping on Peddocks Island
 - The [US Islands Awards Program](https://usislands.org/) and how my Scouts and I
   [qualified Peddocks]({% link _sections/US_Islands_Awards_Program_FirstQ.md %}) for it
 - Four years of trips: [2023]({% link _sections/Peddocks_Island.md %}), [2024]({% link _sections/POTA_Paddocks_Island.md %}),
   [2025]({% link _sections/Back_to_Peddocks_Island.md %}) and [2026]({% link _sections/FT891_at_Peddocks_Island.md %})
 - The gear I bring, and why I recommend staying person-portable: everything fits in a backpack
 - The challenges and rewards of island operating, which go beyond normal field operations
