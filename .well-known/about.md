# Chris Farnham (W1YTQ)

![Chris outside in the winter near a firepit with his dog Daisy](https://journal.seefar.dev/chris_daisy.jpg)

Amateur radio operator, call sign W1YTQ, in Massachusetts. I share my adventures in radio, the outdoors and technology.
I operate CW (Morse code) and SSB from parks for Parks on the Air (POTA), and I build antennas and radio kits.
